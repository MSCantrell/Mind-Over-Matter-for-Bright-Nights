Over Matter: how you get them, what they cost, how they grow, and what can go

Psionic Discipline Atlas (PSIONIC_ATLAS.md, in the mod folder).

 1. QUICK START
===============================================================================

  * Make a NEW world with "Mind Over Matter (BN)" enabled.
  * Pick a psionic profession. Each school has a themed one (list in
    Section 2), or take "Awakening Psion" for a random school.
  * Powers are cast like spells: open your spell menu and pick one. They cost
    STAMINA, not mana.
  * Using your powers levels them up. When the right powers reach the right
    levels, a NEW power arrives on its own with a popup to notify you.
  * Using powers builds NETHER ATTUNEMENT. Past a point, bad things start to
    happen. Let it fade with time, or burn it off with a grounding meditation.
  * INTELLIGENCE and the METAPHYSICS skill are a psion's core stats. Int sets
    how many powers you can hold at once; Metaphysics controls how fast new
    powers arrive.

  >> TIPS:

The 'Mindhunted' start gives a bunch of character creation points- enough to afford
the 'Ascended <psion>' professions, which start with most of their powers 
unlocked and leveled up. The Mindhunter is a serious problem. He'll tank
7.62 and grenades without a problem, and he's got spells that include yoinking 
you down from your roof, tree, vehicle, etc. He's intentionally very hard to kill.
On the other hand, you CAN just drive away. He'll find you again later. 


As you progress, you'll benefit from mutations and bionics that add Int and 
Stamina (yay Respirator!). 


===============================================================================
 2. BECOMING A PSION
===============================================================================

There are eight schools ("paths"). Most characters start with one. You can add
more later, but each extra one is harder to get than the last.

STARTING PROFESSIONS
  Profession                          School           Notes
  ----------------------------------  ---------------  ------------------------
  Star Athlete                        Biokinesis       athletic skills
  Doomseer                            Clairsentience   starts SCHIZOPHRENIC
  Electrical Engineer                 Electrokinesis   Electronics 8, Computers
  Magician                            Photokinesis     Fast Reflexes, Speech
  Firestarter                         Pyrokinesis      starts PYROMANIA
  Test Subject, Project ONYX LIGHTNING Telekinesis     combat skills
  Government Interrogator (DECEASED)  Telepathy        Speech 6
  Itzcuauhtli Corps Liaison           Teleportation    15 points, Metaphysics 7
  Awakening Psion                     random           first crystal certain
  Unwilling Psionic                   random           first crystal certain

  There are also generic "newly / mid / late" versions of each school with
  more starting powers and Metaphysics, plus "random psychic knack"
  professions (see Section 9).

SCENARIOS
  * Challenge - Heart of Fire: a raw, barely controlled pyrokinetic. You will
    never gain a second school.
  * Challenge - Mindhunter: something huge and psychic hunts you, all game.
  * Dimension-Lost: you are a stranded traveler from another Earth.

AWAKENING MID-GAME
  Colored "matrix crystals" turn up in places touched by the Nether
  and on psychic ferals. Wield one and activate it to try to awaken that
  crystal's school. The first crystal is a 55% chance of success. Each school you already
  have roughly halves your odds of getting the next school. See Section 10.


===============================================================================
 3. CASTING POWERS: STAMINA, LEVELS AND FAILURE
===============================================================================

STAMINA IS YOUR MANA
  Every power costs Stamina. Cheap utility powers (Blink, Far Hand, Candle's
  Glow) cost little. Capstones (Crucible, Dilated Gateway, Anabolic
  Rejuvenation) can empty most of your bar. As a power levels up, its cost
  drops (often by half or more) and it usually casts faster too.

  Running out of Stamina mid-fight is a huge problem. Remember that you 
won't be able to do lots of melee and THEN cast an important spell to escape.

POWER LEVELS
  Most powers go up to level 15. A power gains XP each time you cast it, and
  if it's a concentration power then it also gains XP while you hold it. 
Higher levels mean more damage, range and duration, and a lower cost. 
Many powers scale with your Int too. Every power's description says what it does and
  what it leads to.

FAILURE
  Each power has a difficulty. Low Metaphysics plus a difficult power means the
  cast can fail: you pay the cost and get nothing, or worse. Metaphysics rises
  as you use your powers. The meditation recipes in Section 7 help too.

SILENT, HANDS-FREE
  Most powers need no hands and make no casting noise. You can
  use them while grabbed or encumbered.

===============================================================================
 4. CONCENTRATION (MAINTAINED) POWERS
===============================================================================

Powers marked (C) are CONCENTRATION powers. They stay on until you stop
holding them, instead of having a fixed duration. Examples: Personal
Enhancement, Night Eyes, Veil of Light, Lifting Field, Telepathic Shield.

HOW MANY CAN YOU HOLD?
  Your concentration limit is roughly:

      Intelligence / 4   +   concentration bonuses   -   distractions

  So Int 8 holds about 2 powers comfortably and Int 12 about 3. Some powers
  count as more than one. Personal Enhancement, for example, counts as 4
  toward several of the costs below.

  Bonuses:   the Good Concentration trait, the chess-player professions,
             the "concentration" practice recipes (Section 7), and a few
             school traits.
  Penalties: being winded, dazed, downed, nauseous, too hot or cold,
             asthmatic, gassed or hallucinating, mid-study, or carrying
             heavy Nether Attunement.

WHAT HAPPENS AT THE LIMIT
  Casting ANY power while you're at or over your limit forces a
  concentration check. If it fails, "Your concentration breaks!"; you lose
  your held powers and take extra Nether Attunement. The more you were
  holding, the more attunement you take.

ONGOING COSTS
  Every power you hold:
    * burns extra calories,
    * raises the chance each cast adds Nether Attunement (Section 6),
    * makes each attunement gain bigger.

STOPPING
  Use the "Stop Concentrating" power to drop everything, or
  "Stop Concentrating (Individual)" to drop one. Summoned tools tied to a
  concentration power (Hammerhand, the hacking interface, Banked Flames'
  fire, the radio) vanish when you stop holding it. That's intended.


===============================================================================
 5. LEARNING NEW POWERS
===============================================================================

There are no skill books to grind and no 16-hour meditations. Powers
surface on their own.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Every power (apart from your starting ones) has PREREQUISITES: other
     powers at minimum levels. Often there are several alternative routes.
     Example: Oxygen Absorption needs Personal Enhancement 4 + Overcome
     Pain 3.
  2. Once you meet a power's prerequisites, you're ELIGIBLE. The game checks
     about every 15 minutes and may grant one eligible power.
  3. When it does, the game pauses with a "NEW POWER" popup.

  Every power's description ends with lines like:
      "Level 6 leads to Hammerhand with Hardened Skin 6."
  That's your roadmap, with no wiki needed.

HOW FAST?
  The average wait once you're eligible depends on Intelligence and
  Metaphysics:

      P    = (Int - 8) + 1.5 x Metaphysics      (Int below 8 counts as 8)
      wait = about 48 hours - 2.9 hours per point of P   (minimum 1 hour)

    Int  8, Metaphysics 0   ->  about 48 hours
    Int 10, Metaphysics 2   ->  about 34 hours
    Int 12, Metaphysics 4   ->  about 19 hours
    Int 14, Metaphysics 6   ->  about  5 hours
    Int 16+, Metaphysics 6+ ->  about  1 hour (the floor)

  The timer has no memory. Being eligible longer doesn't "fill a bar". Each check 
is a fresh roll at your CURRENT stats. Raising Int or Metaphysics 
speeds it up right away. If several powers are eligible at
  once, one is picked at random.

  >> TIPS (build planning, which routes are fastest):
Another efficient way to pass the time is cast, cast, cast your powers to 
level them up, with "Wait until you catch your breath" each time your stamina runs out. 
Most powers are easy to do this way, like just turning Night Eyes on and off again. 
Some are harder because they require a hostile target, like Oubliette. 


===============================================================================
 6. NETHER ATTUNEMENT: THE PRICE OF POWER
===============================================================================

Psionics draw on the Nether, and the Nether notices. Heavy use builds a
hidden meter, NETHER ATTUNEMENT (0 to 250). Once it is high enough you see a
"Nether-Attunement" status effect, which gets stronger as the meter climbs.

HOW IT RISES
  * Every cast has a chance to add some, shown by the message "you feel a
    strange tingling sensation." The chance goes up with:
      - the power's DIFFICULTY (this counts most: harder powers are much
        riskier),
      - casting repeatedly in quick succession,
      - how many concentration powers you're holding.
  * Holding concentration powers can raise it while you just stand there.
  * A broken concentration adds a lump.
  * Crystalline elixirs raise it the whole time they're active.
  * Contemplation practice adds some.

HOW IT FALLS
  * Naturally: about 1 point every 5 minutes (roughly 12 an hour).
  * Grounding meditation: a craftable "recipe" in the psionic crafting tab
    (Metaphysics 2, 15 minutes, no tools). It vents attunement and briefly
    shields you. You're going to want to add this to the 'favorites' in
    the crafting menu.
  * Incandescent meditation (Heart of Fire only): gaze into a fire for 20
    minutes.
  * Channeling protocol PGS38: learned from a Phavian research report.
    Pours attunement into a drained matrix crystal (Metaphysics 7, 1 hour).
    This is more of a desperate measure. You could do 4 Grounding Meditations
    in the same amount of time and not use up a black crystal. 

WHAT IT DOES TO YOU
  From an attunement of 15, every cast can trigger a CONSEQUENCE. One also
  rolls every ~5 minutes while you're above 15, even if you stop casting, so
  you can't just sit on a full meter. Consequences get nastier the higher you
  go. In rough order of severity:

    Mild:      headaches, nosebleeds, stamina loss, sudden sleepiness,
               vomiting, blurred vision, extra hunger, a cold wind,
               "you feel like you're being watched". Blood loss from the head 
		and hypothermia can get serious if you ignore them.
    Serious:   stabbing feedback pain, mind-shock stun, weakness, fever,
               trouble breathing, a force wave that knocks you flat,
               your powers resisting control, being teleport-locked
    Severe:    a misjump that flings you somewhere unfamiliar, an EMP burst,
               nether lightning, a tear in reality, your powers cut off
               entirely for a while, a MUTATION
    Worst:     "a horrible sensation of watching eyes" -- the Nether's
               hounds come for you or a full RIFT opening nearby

  A few consequences are actually helpful (a surge of strength, a burst of
  vigor). Don't count on them.

  Rule of thumb: keep the meter low when it doesn't matter, so you have room
  to spike it when it does.


===============================================================================
 7. TRAINING: MEDITATION AND CONTEMPLATION
===============================================================================

These live in your crafting menu (practice / psionic tabs) and work like
BN's practice recipes.

MEDITATION (trains Metaphysics)
  meditation (beginner)       Metaphysics 0  -- self-taught at Metaphysics 1,
                                                or from a meditation manual
  meditation (intermediate)   Metaphysics 3  -- intermediate meditation manual
  concentration (beginner)    Metaphysics 2  -- raises your concentration limit
  concentration (expert)      Metaphysics 5  -- raises it further
  thought-form exercises      Metaphysics 4  -- psionic ritual manual
  	neling          Metaphysics 0  -- advanced manual or matrix
                                                schematics
  psionic cultivation         Metaphysics 6  -- advanced psionics manual

  Meditation is "blind-easy" and needs no hands, but pain interferes.

CONTEMPLATION (levels one power without casting it)
  "contemplation: <power>" recipes appear for powers you already know. They
  need a matrix crystal (a drained one works) as a focus. While you
  contemplate, the power gains XP over time (up to a 24-hour session) and
  you train Metaphysics. It adds Nether Attunement. Most uses up a crystal dust.

  It's the only way to level a power you can't safely/easily practice in the field,
  like Gateway, Oubliette, or Reality Tear.

  >> TIPS:

There's often a period in the first day or two where you've established a 
little safety and it's time to heal your wounds and maybe wait for darkness. 
A batch of 6 "concentration (beginner)" recipes unlocks that trait and can 
be a useful way to pass the time. Craft "concentration (expert)" 8 times to 
unlock it.

 8. THE EIGHT SCHOOLS
===============================================================================

(C) = concentration power.  [start] = known as soon as you take up the school.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BIOKINESIS -- the body as an instrument
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Strength, speed, armor and self-healing. The best melee and survival
  school, and since Vitakinesis was folded in, the healer too.

  Personal Enhancement (C) [start]*  +1 to +10 to STR, DEX, INT and PER
                                     together. Scales with level AND Int.
                                     Heavy to hold (counts as 4).
  Overcome Pain (C) [start]          Pain hurts your performance less.
  Healthy Glow (C) [start]           Keeps you healthy; stops asthma attacks.
  Staunch Wound [start]              Closes wounds, stops bleeding.
  Radiation Decontamination [start]  Flushes radiation. Side effects at high
                                     doses.
  Detoxification                     Sweats out poison; your flesh is toxic to
                                     biters meanwhile.
  Oxygen Absorption (C)              Breathe through your skin: underwater, in
                                     thin air (but worse against toxic gas).
  Burst of Speed                     Instantly dash a short distance.
  Hardened Skin (C)                  Skin like armor.
  Adrenaline Trigger                 Speed boost now, crash later.
  Temperature Adaptability (C)       Shrug off heat and cold.
  Enhance Mobility (C)               Ignore much of your encumbrance.
  Hammerhand (C)                     Your hands work as hammer, pry bar, etc.
                                     Can pry doors and pull nails.
  Heightened Reflexes (C)            Increased Speed and Per, and cures temp
				     blindness/deafness. 
  Sealed System                      Nothing gets in or out: gas, poison,
                                     electricity. Cancels Temp. Adaptability
                                     and Oxygen Absorption.
  Metabolic Hyperefficiency (C)      Eat, drink and sleep far less.
  Combat Dance (C)  [capstone]       Big combat boost.
  Perfected Motion (C) [capstone]    Huge movement speed; run across water.
  Hurricane Blows   [capstone]       Several strikes in an instant. Moving
                                     ends it.
  Anabolic Rejuvenation (C) [cap.]   Heal a hundred times faster, fueled by
                                     your body's reserves (eat first).

  >> TIPS:
Personal Enhancement makes everything easier, including your other powers.

Anabolic Rejuvenation is faster than Repair Nanobots, but takes an extreme amount of 
food. Get ready to turn it on and off to eat.

 CLAIRSENTIENCE -- senses beyond the body
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Scouting, information and precision. Weak in a brawl, but you rarely get
  surprised.

  Heightened Senses (C) [start]      See and hear further; later, sense
                                     movement.
  Speed Reader (C) [start]           Read faster and retain more.
  Radiation Sense [start]            Sense radiation on you and around you.
  See Mechanisms*                    Read one adjacent mechanical lock. Doors
                                     still need a lockpick and ~20 seconds,
                                     but the pick CAN'T fail and never trips
                                     alarms. Dial safes open by hand,
                                     instantly. No effect on electronic
                                     locks. Arrives once Heightened Senses
                                     reaches level 3.
  Premonition (C)                    Warning of nearby danger, similar to the
				     Alarm System CBM. 
  Night Eyes (C)                     Like the High Night Vision CBM.
  Aura Sight (C)                     Better persuasion of NPCs.
  Marksman's Eye (C)                 Better ranged range and accuracy.
  Psychometry*                       Read an object's history. More detail at
                                     higher levels. The same item always tells
                                     the same story; it's written into the
                                     item's note.
  Clairvoyance                       See through walls nearby. 
  Combat Sense (C)                   Dodge by glimpsing the next second.
                                     Two bonuses: your dodge skill rises
                                     (+0 at level 1, +4 at level 8, +7 at
                                     15), and you get extra dodge attempts
                                     each turn (+1 at level 1, up to +4 at
                                     15), so a crowd can't wear you down as
                                     fast. It also stops you being grabbed.
  Intuitive Artisan (C)              Craft better, but you're blind to the
                                     world while crafting.
  One Perfect Shot                   One guaranteed-quality ranged attack.
  Satellite View                     Reveal a large area of the map.
  Clarity (C)                        Cures and prevents blind, deaf, stunned, dazed,
				     glare, magical darkness, hallucinations, drunk/high. 
  Prescient Tactician (C)            Your followers dodge much better; you
                                     dodge worse.
  Omniscience       [capstone]       FULL clairvoyance. 

  >> TIPS:

Walking on the ground above labs lets you see where all the armories, etc, are. 
Same with checking for the gun/chem/electronics basements in town.
Clairvoyance has really strong synergies with the Teleport powers.

                                     every body part, on top of your armor.

                                       bash        2.5      6     9.5

buildings. 

Wrecking Ball hits a crowd like a grenade. 


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TELEPATHY -- the mind
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Control, stealth and support. NOTE: zombies, robots, plants, worms and
  slimes have no mind, so they are IMMUNE to telepathic attacks. Play
  telepaths against animals, ferals, humans and Nether things.

  Concentration Trance (C) [start]   Learn faster. Adds +2.5 focus per level
                                     (+37 at level 15) whenever the game
                                     works out how much you learn from
                                     reading, practice or crafting. The
                                     cost: you're nearsighted, walking takes
                                     4x as long, and combat cancels it.
                                     Cast it, then sit down with a book.
  Mind Sonar (C) [start]*            Periodically sense every thinking mind
                                     nearby: direction and distance, even
                                     through walls.
  Telepathic Shield (C)              Protect your mind from psychic attacks.
  Mesmerize                          Put a target in a trance (damage breaks
                                     it).
  Mood Stabilization (C)             Clear bad moods (yours); calm others.
  Synaptic Overload                  Massive brain damage to one target.
  Beastmaster                        Control an animal or feral human.
				     More pets give a compounding combat bonus, up to 8.
  Sensory Deprivation                Blind and confuse a target.
  Primal Terror                      Paralyze a target with fear.
  Obscurity                          Become invisible to chosen minds.
  Psychic Scream                     Hit every mind nearby, friend or foe.
  Beast Tamer                        Make an already-friendly animal stay
                                     friendly far longer.
  Mind Control      [capstone]       Make a target fight for you.
  Network Effect    [capstone]       Link you and your allies for faster
                                     reactions (needs an ally nearby).
                                     "Nearby" means a friendly NPC within
                                     60 tiles on your z-level. You get: +1
                                     Perception (+4 at level 15); +1 extra
                                     dodge per turn (+2 from level 11); +3
                                     tiles of gun range (+6 at 15); +5.5%
                                     ranged damage (+12.5% at 15); and an
                                     "uncanny dodge" against some monster
                                     specials (spit, boomer bile, acid
                                     vomit, a brute's smash) for 100
                                     stamina each. Allies get the level-0
                                     version (+1 Per, +1 dodge, +3 range,
                                     +5% ranged damage, uncanny dodge).

  >> TIPS:
Beastmaster is your key to combat. The Dire Wolf is the premier attacker. Any type
of pet compounds the bonus, so it helps to have a full party even if some are weak.
A Dire Wolf plus 7 squirrels still gets a huge bonus (even though the squirrels
stay wimpy). Watch out for pets that trample each other like moose, bears, and 
sewer gators; they'll hit you too.

                                     it.
  Farstep [start]                    Teleport to a chosen nearby spot.
  Flickerflash Stance [start]        Micro-teleports: harder to pin down,
                                     stronger blows.

===============================================================================
 9. PSYCHIC KNACKS
===============================================================================

A PSYCHIC KNACK is a single wild talent taken from one of the schools (for
example Psychometry, Far Hand, Mesmerize or Banked Flames) without the whole
school behind it. Knacks start at level 6 and don't level up. You can get one
from the "random psychic knack" professions.

If you later awaken the matching school, the knack is replaced by the real,
levelable power. Knacks also make crystal awakenings a little harder (they
count toward the hidden awakening counter).

 10. MATRIX CRYSTALS AND ELIXIRS
===============================================================================

(Full details, with exact odds, are in MATRIX_CRYSTALS.md and
CRYSTALLINE_ELIXIRS.md in the mod folder. Both are spoilers.)

COLORED CRYSTALS -- one per school
  Pink = Biokinesis       Gray   = Clairsentience   Cyan   = Electrokinesis
  Golden = Photokinesis   Red    = Pyrokinesis      Yellow = Telekinesis
  White = Telepathy       Blue   = Teleportation

  Wield the crystal, then activate it. It's used up either way.
    * First school: 55% (certain for Awakening Psion / Unwilling Psionic)
    * Second: 31%   Third: 17%   Fourth: 9.5%   Fifth: about 5%
    * The Noetic Flexibility trait improves every roll by about half again.
    * Failures don't improve your next try; each roll is independent.

  WARNING: activating a crystal for a school you ALREADY HAVE destroys it,
  gives you nothing back, and gives you an hour-long psionic headache.

CORUSCATING CRYSTAL (brown)
  Picks a random school and awakens it, unless you already have that one.
  Odds = (8 - schools you have) / 8. Far better than colored crystals once you
  have two or more schools. It's the most valuable psionic item in the mod.

CRYSTALLINE ELIXIRS
  Brewed from refined crystal dust + clean water (Cooking 5 / Metaphysics 3 to
  learn). Each one gives a powerful 30-hour buff themed to its school. The
  catches:
    * Nether Attunement climbs the WHOLE time you're dosed,
    * a 4-hour comedown follows,
    * non-psions get nothing but sickness.

 11. THE PSIONIC ALLIANCE (mild spoilers)
===============================================================================

Somewhere out there is an old quarry turned into a walled camp of psions.
They welcome awakened psions; the rest are told to behave.

  * The camp's clairsentient keeps a map of psions still lost in the
    wilderness, marked with green pins. Bring them home -- a quest you can
    repeat.
  * The newly awakened are frightened and they hide: in houses (check the
    closets), libraries, banks, churches, motels, caves, by the river, deep
    in the woods. Now and then one is just standing in a field.
  * If you're a psion, let them feel it. If you share their school, you
    have a better way to say "you're not alone" than any speech.
  * Every psion you bring home gets an index card on the green pin map,
    including where you found them. The cards tell the camp's history too.
  * Rescuing a psion of a school UNLOCKS that school's SERVICE in camp:
      Biokinetic       healing, radiation removal, bionic install/removal
      Electrokinetic   recharge every battery you carry, and your bionics
      Telekinetic      repair your damaged gear
      Pyrokinetic      warm you through: clears cold and frostbite
      Photokinetic     fresh vegetables from the basement garden
      Clairsentient    reveal the map around a kind of place you name
      Teleporter       a one-way trip to near one of your map notes
      Telepath         clear bad moods, plus a morale boost
  * Services are on cooldown PER PSION. More psions of a school means more
    help. The camp's original residents don't serve until you've brought home
    someone of their school.

===============================================================================
 12. TROUBLESHOOTING
===============================================================================

  * "Nothing psionic works." Check config/debug.log for the line
        MoM-BN: main loaded (... EOC handlers)
    If it isn't there, the Lua runtime didn't load. You need a recent BN
    nightly with Lua support.
  * "I updated the mod and nothing changed." Quit the game completely and
    relaunch. Loading a save again isn't enough.
  * "A power did nothing." First check the target. Telepathy can't affect the
    mindless; some powers need line of sight. If it really fizzled with no
    message, that's a bug worth reporting.
  * "I haven't learned a new power in ages." Check the prerequisites in your
    powers' descriptions. At low Int/Metaphysics the average wait is about
    2 game days.
  * Reporting bugs: include the mod version (2026.11.7 or later), your BN
    build date, what you did, and config/debug.log.

KNOWN ISSUES (as of this version)
  * Psionic monsters only cast self-buffs once they've spotted you.
  * Maintained-power costs are still being tuned. Feedback welcome.
